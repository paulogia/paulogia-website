Artoo privacy policy

Artoo is a personal dashboard that Paul Ens built for his own use. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody but Paul signs in to it.

When Paul connects his Google account, Artoo reads his Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Tasks. In Gmail it can also create reply drafts, apply its own labels (named R2/...), and remove those labels. In Calendar it can add events that Paul approves. It never sends, archives or deletes email.

The data stays on Paul's own computer, in his home network. Artoo doesn't share it, sell it, or use it for advertising. To summarise threads and draft replies, the text of individual emails is sent to AI services (Anthropic's Claude and TypeSafe's Jev) for that purpose only. Artoo doesn't use Google user data to train AI models, and Paul can revoke its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Artoo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
